Our story

An atelier of cloth, colour & quiet light.

Creations by Anita is a newly launched Amazon India store devoted to pieces that feel personal — Kota Doria sarees with printed pallus, traditional Barmer applique suit sets, and the Bohemian Aroma line of soy-wax candles poured into crystal and wooden jars.

Every listing is made to be gifted or lived with: breathable ethnic wear for festivals and everyday grace, and clean-burning fragrance for living rooms, bedrooms and quiet evenings at home.

Orders are placed securely on Amazon.in with Amazon’s A-to-Z Guarantee. We ship within two days of receiving an order, and most marketplace items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
